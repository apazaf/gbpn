Dear Snowball Warriors,

We promised you it would return, and APAZAF keeps its promises! ❄️🎯 After a long and painful year without a single snowball thrown in anger, we are absolutely THRILLED to announce that the **2027 Great Snowball Fight** will take place on **Saturday, 6 February 2027**!

Yes, you read that right: **February**. For the first time in our millenary history, the battle is leaving January behind. Why? Because the snow gods have been stingy lately, and January has kept handing us more grass than ammunition. A few extra weeks of winter mean deeper snow, better packing and bigger forts. We're not breaking with tradition, we're just following the snow! 🏔️

Two years of waiting have passed since the last battle. Your arms are rested, your grudges are fresh, and your fort-building plans have had plenty of time to mature. It's time to put them to the test! 🥶

Stay tuned for all the frosty updates by following our [news](/post) section or joining our [Telegram group](https://t.me/joinchat/UsNhFbmVl6W_Odyz). Details on [registration](/register) and logistics are coming soon!

In the meantime, start your winter training regimen:

- Practice your throws with any spherical object (APAZAF disclaims any responsibility for damage to persons or property)
- Build up your snow fort engineering skills
- Stock up on your best hot chocolate for post-battle celebrations
- Update your calendar: it's **February** this time, don't show up in January! 📅

Remember: no yellow snow, no ice chunks, and definitely no rocks disguised as snowballs. We're warriors, not barbarians!

Snow-fully yours,
The APAZAF Team ⛄
